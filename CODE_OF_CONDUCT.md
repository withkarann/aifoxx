# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AIFOXX is built in public and open to everyone. We aim to keep this a
respectful, safe, and productive space.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers. Everyone starts somewhere.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, intimidation, or abusive language.
- Sharing someone's private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations of this
policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns via GitHub Discussions or a private Security Advisory
thread: https://github.com/withkarann/aifoxx
